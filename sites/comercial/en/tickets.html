How the line works

Three things the protocol already does.

01 · Listing

You publish price and capacity

Title, face value and capacity. Once tickets are sold, that price stays fixed, so a return is still the same amount.

02 · Purchase

Up to four per person

Each purchase pays face value, not one peso more inside the contract. It can be transferred once, as a gift or up to that price. A second transfer does not exist.

03 · Door

The signature has to be the holder's

The camera reads cigo:boleto:…. If the signature is not the current holder's, they do not enter. If they already went through, the screen says so and the ticket can no longer be transferred or returned.

The settlement

What a ticketing company keeps, and what this box office does not take.

For halls of 500 to 3,000 people, the hit is usually a 12% to 18% fee and money that arrives weeks later. Here the buyer pays face value. The contract does not keep a percentage. The withdrawal is made by whoever created the show, when the window closes.

If they do not go

Before going through, the ticket can return to the box office. The buyer gets their share back and the seat returns to sale. After the door, there is no transfer and no return.

Who marks the entry

The account that created the show, or an address on the list it built. Another wallet cannot mark a ticket for that show as entered.
